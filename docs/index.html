Banner Plus

Banner has the data. It has no screen for the questions you actually ask it — so this adds them, inside Banner, on the session you are already signed in to.

Banner Plus
Drag this to your bookmarks bar, open Banner Faculty Self-Service, and click it.
⌘⇧B shows the bar if it is hidden.

What it does

  • Your classesEvery section you teach in a term, with enrolment counts, in one list. Or every term at once.
  • Rosters of facesA grid of photographs you can actually learn names from, or a sortable table when you are reading rather than recognising.
  • Student recordsPhotograph, major, standing, this term's schedule with rooms and times, and the full registration history laid out as a transcript — seasons across, years down, newest first.
  • Shared free timeSelect any set of students and see when they are collectively not in class. Hover a slot for who is free and who is not.
  • GroupsAn artificial class of students who share no section — a research group, your advisees. Search by name, paste UINs, or drag students in from a roster.
  • Printable sheetsA photo roster with colour-coded majors, auto-fitted to the page, and a free-time sheet.
Everything runs in your browser. No student data is sent anywhere — this page is static and has no server behind it. What it produces is a named list of students with photographs and grades; treat it the way you would treat a gradebook.
